about me

well, officially it’s caitlin, but kye just fits better! endlessly curious, a little creative, and always chasing something new to learn. when i’m not working, i’m behind a camera chasing natural light and the funny, unplanned moments that make the best stories. i’m also a proud hobby collector: cooking, sewing, scrapbooking, jiu-jitsu, and books i swear i’ll only “skim.” some stick, some don’t, but the fun’s in the trying.

that same curiosity shaped my career. i studied management information systems, IT management, and accounting through my BS, MS, and executive MBA, not to stack degrees, but to understand how business and tech work together to solve real problems. i love strategy, systems, and creativity equally, especially when there’s chaos to turn into order (sometimes with just a spreadsheet). growth is the goal: i experiment, learn by doing, and try not to take myself too seriously.
